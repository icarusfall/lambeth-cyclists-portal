Have we said anything about Streatham High Road before?

Yes — twice, both times about the same stretch north of the station.

March 2026 — we responded to the bus priority scheme. We supported the bus lane but asked for the cycle lane to be kept at 2.0m past the pinch point at Gleneagle Road, and objected to the shared-use footway outside the station.

October 2025 — a traffic order for loading bays. We didn’t object.

The current TfL consultation covers much the same ground, and the 1.2m pinch point we flagged in March appears to still be there.
