Lambeth Council consultation Needs a second reader

Brixton Hill / Effra Road junction improvements

Closes in 11 days — Sunday 14 September. We want ours in by the 12th so there is room to fix anything.

What this is

The council wants to rebuild the junction where Effra Road and Brixton Water Lane meet Brixton Hill. Here is what stood out when we read the documents:

—

Separate signal stages for people cycling, so you no longer turn across traffic.

—

The northbound cycle lane narrows to 1.2m on the approach — below the London standard.

—

A left-turn slip lane is kept, with a shared surface across it.

—

Two bus stops move; no cycle parking is proposed anywhere in the scheme.

Our draft response

First drafted from the consultation documents, then edited by Colin on 2 September. Nobody else has read it yet.

Lambeth Cyclists welcomes the rebuilding of this junction. It is one of the more intimidating places to ride in the borough, and separate signal stages for cycling are a real improvement. We support the scheme, with three points we would ask the council to look at again.

1. Lane width on the northbound approach

The lane narrows to 1.2m exactly where riders are being overtaken by buses. Where the carriageway allows, we would ask for 2.0m, and for the narrowing to be moved away from the pinch point.

2. The left-turn slip lane

Retaining the slip with a shared surface puts turning drivers and people cycling in the same space at the point of conflict. We would prefer the slip removed; failing that, a tightened radius so turns are taken slowly.

3. Cycle parking

The scheme proposes none. Given the shops on Effra Road, we would ask for at least six Sheffield stands within the scheme boundary.

We would be glad to talk any of this through with officers.
